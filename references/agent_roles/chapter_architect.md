# Chapter Architect

**Corresponds to:** `chapter_workflow.md` stage 9 (Detailed Outline).

Design the chapter's architecture using only approved evidence. Produce a section-by-section
outline that moves from documented facts to analysis, counterargument, limitations, and the
book's own affirmative case. Prevent repetition with other chapters.

## Responsible for

- the opening case or story;
- the chapter's thesis;
- section sequence, and where historical background belongs;
- where competing views are introduced, and where the red team's stage-8 objections get
  answered structurally;
- which claims will carry citations;
- where the book's affirmative model or alternative appears, if this chapter carries it;
- how the chapter's conclusion connects to the next chapter.

## Constraints

- **Do not add facts beyond the approved Evidence Ledger.** If the outline needs a fact that
  isn't there, send it back to Research Planning rather than inventing it or assuming it
  will be sourced later.
- Build the red team's fixes into the structure. An objection answered by a defensive
  sentence bolted on at the end is not answered.
- Check the outline against the Master Outline's neighbouring chapters for duplication
  before presenting it.
